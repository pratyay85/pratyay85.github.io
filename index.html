Hi! I’m Pratyay Mukherjee. Welcome to my academic (and non-academic) webpage!  In my native language Bengali my name is written as প্রত্যয় মুখার্জী.

Research & Background

Currently I am working as a Principal Researcher at Hashgraph. I am also a Visiting Scientist / Guest Faculty at Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata. Moreover, I am the founder and director of AlgoBengal Research Labs.

Earlier I was a Senior Director of Research at Supra Research from June 2022 to September 2025; a Senior Research Scientist at Swirlds Labs from January 2022 to June 2022; a Research Scientist (and later a Senior Research Scientist) at Visa Research from March 2017 to January 2022; and a Post-doctoral Researcher at EECS, UC Berkeley hosted by Sanjam Garg during Fall 2015–Spring 2017 where I was affiliated with Crypto and Theory research groups. My research encompasses all aspects of Cryptography and Security. Recently I have been mostly focusing on applications of Cryptography in the Payment and Blockchain space.

In October 2015 I received my PhD in Computer Science from Aarhus University, Denmark, where I was a PhD Fellow (August 2012 – August 2015) supervised by Jesper Buus Nielsen at the Crypto Group led by Ivan Damgård. I spent the final year of my PhD at Northeastern University, Boston visiting Daniel Wichs. I obtained my Master of Technology in Computer Science from Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur (in 2011) supervised by Abhijit Das and Bachelor of Engineering in Electronics and Telecom. from Jadavpur University, Kolkata (in 2008).

I am also the founder and host of the podcast The Coffee House Experience.
