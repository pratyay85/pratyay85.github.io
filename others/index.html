Films

I made a short film called The Return. The film is in Bengali, with English subtitles. Watch it on YouTube. The film is also featured in SHORTED. My independent film production venture is called Humanist Cinema.

Here’s the log-line:

In California’s Silicon Valley, at a usual family party folks get quite nostalgic while arguing about the prospects of returning to their native place Kolkata in India. The party takes an unusual turn when an unexpected guest arrives.

Education For All

  • Rokeya Shiksha Kendra

    Since 2024, I have been supporting Rokeya Shiksha Kendra, a volunteer-led community learning centre in Patuli, South Kolkata. It offers holistic education to children in the local community, bringing academic support together with creative and cultural activities. I warmly encourage everyone to visit its Instagram page and support its work in any way they can.

  • Kolotan

    Since 2025, I have been supporting Kolotan, an alternative school in Santoshpur–Mirhati, a village near Barasat. Inspired by Rabindranath Tagore’s approach to education, it combines academic learning with art, music, and a close connection to nature. Please visit its website to learn more about its activities and, if possible, support its work.

  • Padakshep

    From 2013 to 2023, I volunteered with Padakshep, a non-profit organization supporting talented students from financially disadvantaged backgrounds in West Bengal. Please consider donating or joining the effort. You can also learn about its scholars and projects.
