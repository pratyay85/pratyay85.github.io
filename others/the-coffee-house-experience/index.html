I founded The Coffee House Experience in October 2020. It is a bilingual podcast in Bengali and English, where I host conversations with co-hosts and guests on a wide range of topics.

The tone is informal, in the spirit of the Bengali tradition of adda.

Watch & Listen

Watch the show on YouTube, or listen to the audio edition on your preferred podcast platform.

Behind the Name

The name draws on Kolkata’s College Street Coffee House and its association with adda. Explore the connection through this song about the Coffee House. If you don’t understand Bengali, here is an English translation of the lyrics.
