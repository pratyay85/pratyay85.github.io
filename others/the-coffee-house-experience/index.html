I started this bilingual (Bengali/English) podcast on YouTube to discuss a wide range of topics that excite me and like-minded people. The discussions are kept at an informal level (a.k.a. Adda in Bengali). If you’re wondering about the name of the channel, please visit the College Street Coffee House and explore its connection with Adda 😉 (if you don’t understand Bengali, I found a decent translation of the lyrics for you).
